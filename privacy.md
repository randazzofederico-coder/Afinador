Tuner Privacy Policy. Last updated: 11/01/2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tuner (Afinador, part of the Saroo Apps ecosystem) handles your information when you use our mobile, desktop or web (PWA) application.

By using Tuner, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Summary: Tuner does not require an account, does not collect personal data and never sends your audio anywhere.

Audio Processing and Microphone Use. Tuner is a real-time pitch detection tool that requires access to your device's microphone in order to work.
Local Processing: Audio captured by the microphone is processed strictly locally on your device using frequency detection algorithms (YIN algorithm).

No recording or transmission: The application does not record, store or transmit any audio or voice fragment to our servers or to third parties. The audio stream is discarded immediately after calculating the frequency needed to display the tuning.

Information We Collect. Tuner does not ask you to sign up or sign in, and does not collect personal data such as your name, email address or location.

Local Storage (Preferences). Tuner uses your device's local storage only to remember your usage preferences:
The reference pitch (e.g. 440 Hz or 432 Hz), the selected instrument transposition, the keep-screen-on preference, the selected microphone (on desktop and web) and the app language.

This data stays on your device and is never sent to any server. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

Third-Party Services. The web version of Tuner is served through Firebase Hosting (Google). Like any web server, this service may log technical connection data (such as IP address and browser type) for security and operational purposes. We recommend reviewing Google's privacy policy to understand how this information is handled.

Children. Tuner can be used by people of any age, since it does not collect personal data.

Changes to this Privacy Policy. We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page or within the application. You are advised to review this Policy periodically for any changes.

Contact. If you have any questions, concerns or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us through our official website: https://federicorandazzo.com.ar/apps/
