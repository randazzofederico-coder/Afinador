Short Description
Real-time chromatic tuner with instrument transposition, Hz calibration and a visual tuning seismograph.

Full Description
High-precision tuning: Captures audio in real time and uses the YIN algorithm to detect the fundamental frequency, identifying the closest note (C0-B8) and showing the exact deviation in cents (-50 to +50).
Built-in transposition: Automatically adjusts the on-screen reading for transposing instruments, such as Bb or Eb clarinets and saxophones, and F horns.
Custom A4 calibration: Lets you set the reference pitch between 400 and 480 Hz (for example, 432 Hz or 442 Hz), saving your preference automatically.
Visual meters: Features a color-coded cents needle (green for in tune, yellow for close, red for out of tune) and a "seismograph" that draws a smooth history of the latest readings so you can judge how stable your sound is.
Optimized performance: Processes audio in the background to keep the screen smooth at 60 fps, and includes a filter that smooths the visual reading so it doesn't look jittery.
Works offline: No account or internet connection required; all processing happens on your device.
Cross-platform: Works on Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, Linux and web browsers (as an installable PWA).
Practical tools: Includes an option to keep the device screen on while you practice, and lets you choose a specific microphone on desktop computers and the web.
Multilingual: Available in English and Spanish.
